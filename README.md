### 🐟 [LSTM vs GRU — Water Quality Prediction](https://github.com/Nicolauzzz/lstm-gru-water-quality-prediction)
> Comparing LSTM & GRU deep learning models for predicting water quality (Temperature, pH, DO) in catfish farming ponds — 30 minutes ahead.

`Python` `TensorFlow` `Time Series` `Deep Learning` `IoT`

**Results:** GRU WS=90 achieved **MAE 0.1388** with **R² 0.9133** for temperature and **R² 0.8762** for dissolved oxygen.
